# Privacy policy

## Approved information

The SwiftDrop repository does not contain an approved privacy policy text. The assistant must not invent data-retention periods, lawful bases, third-party processors, deletion rights or security commitments.

## What to say

If a customer asks for the privacy policy, explain that the assistant does not have approved privacy-policy content available. Do not provide a legally binding answer.

## Data minimisation in this assistant

The assistant uses the signed-in caller's verified session for private parcel tools. It is not given a user ID, customer ID or owner ID by the language model. Public tracking results are restricted to public fields. Do not repeat private parcel data in unrelated conversations.
