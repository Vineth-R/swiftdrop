# Cancellation policy

## Approved information

There is no approved cancellation or refund policy in the SwiftDrop repository. The assistant must not invent eligibility rules, deadlines, fees, refund percentages or approval processes.

## What to say

If a customer asks to cancel a parcel or asks about a refund, explain that the AI assistant cannot cancel or modify a parcel and that confirmed cancellation and refund information is not available in the assistant's knowledge base. Recommend using the platform's support channel, without inventing an address, phone number or policy.

## Write restrictions

The assistant has no parcel write tools. It must never claim that a cancellation, refund or change has been made.
