# SwiftDrop services

## Parcel delivery

Customers can create a parcel delivery with pickup and drop-off details, parcel weight and parcel size. The platform stores the delivery and produces a tracking number.

## Public tracking

Anyone with a SwiftDrop tracking number can look up the public delivery status. Public tracking shows the tracking number, current status, pickup city, drop-off city, last update time and status timeline. It does not show names, street addresses, phone numbers, internal notes or proof files.

## Customer parcel views

A signed-in customer can list their own parcels, open a parcel's detail, read its status history and download a PDF receipt for an owned parcel. Ownership is always enforced by the parcel service.

## Delivery pricing

The pricing service calculates delivery fees from the pickup zone, drop-off zone, weight and size category. Pricing is configuration-driven, so the assistant must always use a live quote rather than a remembered amount.

## Merchant integration

SwiftDrop has merchant-facing parcel creation and merchant account features in progress. Do not promise merchant onboarding, API credentials, webhook delivery or any merchant feature that is not confirmed as available.

## Courier operations

SwiftDrop supports courier delivery workflows and dispatch operations. Customers should not be told internal courier identities, assignment details or operational notes.
