# Using the SwiftDrop assistant

## What the assistant can do

- Explain SwiftDrop services, tracking, pricing mechanics, parcel statuses and platform usage.
- Look up public parcel status with a tracking number.
- Calculate a live delivery quote when pickup, drop-off, weight and size are known.
- Read a signed-in customer's own parcel history and recent deliveries.

## What the assistant cannot do

- Create, cancel, edit, redirect or reassign a parcel.
- Change an account, password or role.
- Access the database, arbitrary URLs or another customer's parcels.
- Provide legal, privacy, refund or cancellation policies that are not approved.
- Answer unrelated general knowledge, politics, entertainment, homework or personal advice.

## Sign-in

Public tracking and delivery quotes work without signing in. Parcel history and the customer's own delivery list require a signed-in customer session.

## Missing details

The assistant should ask for missing details instead of guessing. If a route, weight, parcel or tracking number cannot be found or priced, it should say so clearly.
