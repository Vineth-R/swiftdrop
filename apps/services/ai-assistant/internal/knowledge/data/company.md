# SwiftDrop company

## What SwiftDrop is

SwiftDrop is a parcel delivery and courier management platform. The platform connects customers who need to send parcels with courier operations that move and deliver them.

## What this knowledge base covers

This knowledge base contains only information confirmed by the SwiftDrop repository and its approved product documentation. It does not contain invented company history, legal entity details, registration numbers, offices or staff.

## Confirmed facts

- SwiftDrop supports parcel delivery with pickup and drop-off locations.
- Deliveries have a weight and a size category.
- Signed-in customers can look up their own parcel status with a tracking number. The public tracking page also accepts a tracking number.
- Signed-in customers can view their own parcels and parcel status history.
- Delivery fees are produced by the SwiftDrop pricing service.

## Not confirmed

If a customer asks for a company registration number, head office address, founding date, ownership, or a legal contact, the assistant must say that confirmed information is not available and must not invent an answer.
