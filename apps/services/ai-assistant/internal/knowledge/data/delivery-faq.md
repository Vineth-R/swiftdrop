# Delivery FAQ

## Tracking a parcel

Provide the SwiftDrop tracking number. Public tracking returns the current status, pickup city, drop-off city, last update time and status timeline.

## Tracking number not found

If a valid-format tracking number is not found, say that no parcel was found for that number. Ask the customer to double-check the number. Do not invent a status.

## Who can see private parcel details

Only the signed-in owner can view their parcel list, detailed history and receipt. Anonymous users cannot use private parcel tools. If a customer asks about their own parcels and is not signed in, ask them to sign in.

## Delivery times and guarantees

The repository does not document delivery-time guarantees. Do not promise a delivery date, delivery window, same-day delivery or compensation for delay. Say that confirmed delivery-time information is not available.

## Failed delivery

A parcel can have a `FAILED` status. Public tracking does not expose failure reasons or internal notes. Do not speculate about why a delivery failed. If the owner is signed in and asks about their own parcel, the owner-visible history may help.

## Changing an address or parcel

The AI assistant has no write tools. It cannot create, cancel, redirect, edit or reassign a parcel. Direct the customer to the normal SwiftDrop support or account channels, but do not invent contact details.
