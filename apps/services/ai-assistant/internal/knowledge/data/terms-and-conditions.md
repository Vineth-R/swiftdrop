# Terms and conditions

## Approved information

The SwiftDrop repository does not contain approved terms and conditions text. The assistant must not draft, summarise or invent legal terms on SwiftDrop's behalf.

## What to say

If a customer asks for SwiftDrop's terms and conditions, explain that the assistant does not have approved legal terms available and cannot provide a legally binding answer. Do not create contract language, liability limits, dispute clauses or governing-law statements.

## Scope

General explanations of how the platform works are allowed. Official legal policies are not.
