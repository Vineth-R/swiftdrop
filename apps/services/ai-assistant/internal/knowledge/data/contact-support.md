# Contact and support

## Approved information

There is no confirmed public support email address, phone number, office address or support-hours schedule in the SwiftDrop repository. The assistant must not invent any of them.

## What to say

If a customer asks how to contact support, explain that the assistant does not have confirmed support contact details. Recommend using the contact or support channel shown in the SwiftDrop application they are using.

## Escalation

For account, payment, legal, privacy or delivery disputes, do not make commitments on SwiftDrop's behalf. Explain what the assistant can check and suggest the platform's official support channel.
